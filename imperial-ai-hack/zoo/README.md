# Opponent zoo

Stand-ins for what other teams are likely to upload. Every file is self-contained
(class `Agent`, standard library only, a few microseconds to ~0.3 ms per decision) and
deterministic: its dice are re-seeded every hand from its seat, the hand number and its
own hole cards. Generated by `tools/build_zoo.py` from `tools/zoo_src/`; edit there.

`tier` is what the gauntlet uses to blend a table: fish = big donators, medium = the
bulk of a student field, strong = reference bots that punish candidates which only
beat fish.

| file | tier | archetype | what it does | leak to exploit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `limp_caller.py` | fish | limp-caller (loose passive) | Limps 65% of hands from every seat, calls raises up to 4bb with the better 40%, never raises without aces or kings; after the flop it checks, calls up to three-quarter pot with any pair or draw and folds air. | Limps then calls: isolate with 4-5bb raises in position, c-bet (it folds air on the flop), then value bet pairs relentlessly; when it bets it has two pair or better. |
| `maniac.py` | fish | maniac | Raises about 80% of hands preflop to 4-6bb, re-raises with almost half of them, pots it on every street and raises bets at random. | Its bets and raises carry almost no information: 3-bet/4-bet for value much wider, call down with any decent pair, check strong hands to it and let it barrel; never bluff it. |
| `minraise.py` | fish | min-raise spammer | Min-raise spammer: min-raises preflop with 60% of hands (and re-min-raises), min-bets every time it is checked to, min-raises bets with any pair or draw, otherwise calls small bets. | Bloats pots at a tiny price with weak ranges and then calls too much: re-raise big for value over its min-raises, call its min-bets with any equity, and value bet large; its raise sizes say nothing about strength. |
| `pot_odds.py` | fish | pot-odds bot (equity vs random hand) | Pure pot-odds caller: estimates its equity against ONE random hand (table preflop, 90-rollout Monte Carlo after the flop) and calls exactly when equity beats the pot odds; bets half pot only above 80% equity, never bluffs. | Measures itself against a random hand, not your range: it limps and calls raises with 80% of hands, calls 100bb jams with any above-average hand and pays off bets with bottom pair or ace high. Value bet big and often, never bluff less than an overbet, fold when it bets. |
| `shove_loose.py` | fish | preflop shove-bot (top 40%) | Push-or-fold bot: moves all in preflop with the top 40% of hands, folds the rest, and shoves any pair or better after the flop. | Jams 40% of hands for 100bb: call with the top 17-18% (66+, A8s+, ATo+, KTs+, KQo) and print; never raise-fold in front of it, and do not open hands that cannot call its shove when it is still to act. |
| `station_any.py` | fish | calling station (calls anything) | Calls any bet of any size on any street with any two cards, never folds; bets a third of the pot with two pair or better and min-raises only with a straight or better. | Never folds: zero bluffs, value bet three streets as big as possible with top pair or better (it even calls all-ins), isolate it preflop with big raises; believe its rare raise. |
| `station_cap.py` | fish | calling station (folds only to huge bets) | Calls unless the bet is huge: sees every flop for up to 15bb, calls bets up to pot size with any pair, draw or ace high, and folds to overbets and preflop all-ins without a strong hand. | Pays off every bet up to pot size with weak pairs and ace high: value bet thin at 75-100% pot on all three streets, never bluff below an overbet; its bets (two pair+) are honest. |
| `barreler.py` | medium | over-bluffing barreler | Over-bluffing barreler: opens 30%, then fires 75% pot on the flop, 80% on the turn and pot on the river almost regardless of its cards whenever it is checked to; folds to raises without top pair. | Three barrels with air far too often: check to it and call down with any reasonable pair, or raise the turn (it folds everything below top pair). Do not lead into it, let it bet. |
| `cbet_fold.py` | medium | c-bet always, fold to raise | Opens the top 22% from any seat for 3bb, always c-bets two-thirds pot on the flop, then shuts down without top pair and folds to any raise without two pair. | One-and-done: raise its flop c-bet with anything or float and bet when it checks the turn; 3-bet its opens (it folds all but QQ+/AK). Respect a second barrel. |
| `fit_or_fold.py` | medium | top-pair-or-better fit-or-fold | Sees cheap flops with 45% of hands (raises only the top 5%), then continues only with top pair or better or a strong draw: bets three-quarter pot, calls anything, raises sets; otherwise check-folds. | Misses two flops in three and gives up: c-bet every flop small and take it; when it calls or bets it has top pair or better, so stop bluffing and only continue with hands that beat top pair. |
| `lag.py` | medium | loose-aggressive | Loose-aggressive: opens 22-60% by position, 3-bets light, c-bets and barrels most boards, floats and semi-bluff-raises draws. | Too many bluffs and too many light calls: 3-bet and 4-bet it for value wider, call down with top pair and good second pair, value bet big; do not try to bluff it off pairs. |
| `mc_threshold.py` | medium | Monte Carlo equity vs random + thresholds | The quickly written Monte Carlo bot: equity against random hands (one per live opponent, capped at two) and fixed thresholds - raise above 0.70, call when equity beats pot odds, fold otherwise; preflop it raises the top 13% and calls anything whose equity vs a random hand clears the price. | Equity vs random hands over-values weak made hands against betting ranges: it calls two streets with second pair and raises top pair into anything. Value bet thinner and bigger, expect it to fold to very large bets without 60% raw equity, and note it never bluffs or bluff-raises - fold to its raises without a strong hand. Preflop it calls opens with ~65% of hands. |
| `nit.py` | medium | nit | Plays about 8% of hands (77+, AJs+, AQo+, KQs), raises them 3bb, bets top pair or better and gives up to any real pressure. | Folds 92% of hands and most flops: steal its blinds every time, c-bet small, and fold at once when it raises or keeps betting (it has it). |
| `shove_tight.py` | medium | preflop shove-bot (top 12%) | Push-or-fold bot: moves all in preflop with the top 12% of hands (44+, A5s+, ATo+, KTs+, QJs), folds everything else, and only continues after the flop with top pair or better (by shoving). | Call its jams with exactly the hands that beat a 12% range (about 88+, AJs+, AKo) and fold the rest; its blinds are free to steal 88% of the time. |
| `tag_charts.py` | medium | rule-based TAG (position charts) | Textbook rule-based TAG: opening chart by position (9% under the gun to 32% on the button), 3-bets JJ+/AK, c-bets when it connects or heads-up, then plays fit-or-fold. | Static and honest: folds to 3-bets without a premium, gives up the turn without top pair, never bluff-catches a raise. Float or raise its c-bets, 3-bet its late opens, fold to its turn and river bets. |
| `trapper.py` | medium | slow-playing trapper | Slow-player: limps or flat-calls its premiums, checks and calls two pair or better on the flop, check-raises the turn and jams the river; with one pair it bets a third of the pot as a blocker. | Its small bets are weak one-pair hands (raise them), its checks hide monsters: do not barrel into a player who check-called the flop, and fold to its turn check-raise. Take the free cards it gives. |
| `ref_lag.py` | strong | strong reference: balanced-ish aggressive, range-aware equity | Strong reference 2, a balanced-ish aggressive bot: wide position opens (14% early to 52% on the button), polarised 3-bets with suited bluffs, and after the flop a range-aware equity model - Monte Carlo equity vs a random hand, re-scaled against the share of hands each opponent still holds given his actions and measured frequencies - driving value bets, mixed-frequency bluffs with the betting lead, check-raise traps and a minimum-defence-frequency backstop so it cannot be barrelled off the top of its range. | Its bluff frequency follows your measured fold-to-bet: call it down a few times early and the bluffs dry up; it opens wide, so 3-bet it for value in position and it folds most of that range; its range model treats every bet from an unknown player the same, so unusual lines (small donk bets, check-raises with air) cost it chips in the first ~30 hands. |
| `ref_tag.py` | strong | strong reference: solid TAG with action-based hand reading | Strong reference 1, a solid adaptive TAG: position opening ranges (10% early to 44% on the button), value-heavy 3-bets, calls shoves by the shover's measured frequency, steals more from tight blinds; after the flop it sizes by board texture and by how sticky the opponents are, and reads hands by action (folds one pair to raises and triple barrels from passive players, calls down wild ones). | No easy money: it needs ~30 hands to adapt, its small c-bet on dry boards is often air (raise it occasionally), it over-folds one pair to turn/river raises from players it has tagged as passive, and it rarely bluffs rivers - fold bluff-catchers to its river bets. Stay near baseline against it. |

The kit's three house bots (`examples/calling_station.py`, `random_bot.py`,
`tight_aggressive.py`) join the pool with `--field mixed` or `--field house`.
